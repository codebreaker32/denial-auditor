**Notes for honest use:**
- The 13% OIG figure comes from a sample of 250 denials by 15 large MA plans in one week of June 2019. Say "a 2022 federal audit found…", not "13% of all denials".
- The KFF Marketplace figures cover HealthCare.gov non-group plans only, not employer plans.
- The 80.7% overturn rate counts appeals that were made. It doesn't mean 80% of denials are wrong; it means the few that are appealed usually win. That is the gap this product targets.
- 422.101 is decision-support context, not a legal conclusion: say what the LCD for the date requires, never that a plan's denial was unlawful.
- Our own numbers (30/30 and so on) come from synthetic cases with our own ground truth. Always say so.
