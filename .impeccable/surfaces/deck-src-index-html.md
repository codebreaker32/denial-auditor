## Direction contract

THESIS: Each slide is one sheet of the claim form at poster scale: drop-out red printed structure (heading bar, numbered field boxes, model/code checkboxes), one black statement, one real screenshot of the app, qualifiers as footnotes. It refuses the dark slide, the headline-plus-bullets slide and the stat-tile grid.

OWN-WORLD: White sheet, drop-out red #C8262B for the heading bar, box numbers and condensed caps labels, line-red for field rules, typed black #141414. Archivo condensed 800 for statements, Archivo for prose, Courier Prime Bold for typed figures and IDs (80.7%, 30/30, $0.0596). The checked box (red square, typed black X) marks verified and "model/code".

STORY: Judges see the gap (80.7% of appealed MA prior-auth denials overturned, 11.5% appealed), follow one synthetic denial through nine numbered steps where each box is ticked model or code, watch one changed word lock the export, read 30/30 with its honest scope, and leave with the URL.

FORM: Red drop-out claim form (CMS-1500), pick card, seed e6dbdb1e. Signature: the pipeline sheet, nine numbered boxes each ticked ☒ MODEL / ☒ CODE with its cost typed in.
